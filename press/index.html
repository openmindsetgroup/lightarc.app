Lightarc press kit

In one paragraph

Lightarc is a weight trend tracker for Android, made by OMS, a small independent studio. It turns day-to-day weigh-ins into one calm trend line, so people can see the direction they're heading instead of reacting to a single morning's number. It also has optional, free GLP-1 dose logging with private reminders, injection-site rotation and a side-effect log. There's no account and no ads, and your data stays on your phone.

What it does

  • The trend, not the daily number. A moving average of daily weigh-ins that handles missed days, with three smoothing settings, a weekly rate, change since start and an optional goal.
  • Bring your history. Imports Libra, Happy Scale and Shotsy CSV files and most other apps' CSV files, with a preview before anything is saved. Can read weight from Health Connect.
  • GLP-1 logging, free. Weekly, daily or every-few-days doses; reminders with Taken and Snooze that never name the medication unless the user chooses; injection-site rotation; a side-effect log; dose days and on-medication periods marked on the chart.
  • Your data, yours. No account, no server, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The only network use is Google Play Billing for the optional purchase. Export a full backup or CSV any time. Android Backup to the user's own Google account is on by default and can be turned off.
  • Premium (one-time): Insights, 6-month and 1-year views with pan and zoom, goal prediction as a date range, Stats, milestones, a home-screen widget, more than one medication at a time, and a regain alert.

What it isn't

Lightarc is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and it never gives medical advice. Its estimated medication level is an illustration from published prescribing information, not a measurement. Lightarc is for adults aged 18 and over.

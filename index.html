Your weight trend, not the daily noise.

Lightarc smooths out day-to-day swings from water, salt and meals into one calm trend line, so you can see where you're actually heading. A weight trend tracker for Android, with optional GLP-1 dose logging built in.

  • No account
  • No ads

Free, with an optional one-time Premium unlock. No subscription.

The trend, clearly

  • A smoothed trend line over your daily weigh-ins, with three smoothing settings
  • Weekly rate, change since you started, and progress to an optional goal
  • Tap +, set your weight, save. Several weigh-ins a day are fine
  • A daily or weekly weigh-in reminder, if you want one

Bring your history with you

  • Import from Libra, Happy Scale or Shotsy, from most other apps' CSV files, or from a Lightarc backup
  • Have a smart scale? Lightarc can read the weigh-ins its app saves in Health Connect (weight only, read-only)
  • You see a preview before anything is saved

How to move your weight history

GLP-1 and other medications, built in and free

  • Weekly injections, daily pills or every-few-days schedules: log a dose with the amount and a note
  • Injection-site rotation, with your last site shown and the next one suggested
  • Reminders with Taken and Snooze, worded discreetly, that never name your medication unless you choose to
  • Dose days, dose changes and on-medication periods marked on your weight chart
  • A side-effect log, for your own record or your prescriber

Doses and weight on one chart

  • See your whole history on the chart for free

Keeping your GLP-1 doses and your weight in one place

If you take a GLP-1 medication, you may be keeping two logs: one for doses and one for your weight. Lightarc puts both on one chart, so your own record sits in one place, ready for you or your prescriber to look at.

Lightarc is a weight trend tracker at heart. The medication log is optional, built in and free.

What you can log

  • Doses. Weekly injections, daily pills, or every-few-days schedules. You type the amount you were prescribed; Lightarc never suggests one.
  • Dose changes. When the amount changes, you choose "Make it regular" or "Just this once", and the chart marks the change.
  • Injection sites. Abdomen, thigh or arm, left or right. Lightarc shows your last site and preselects the next one in the rotation.
  • How you feel. Tag side effects such as nausea or tiredness, add a note, and link it to a dose if you like.
  • On and off periods. Mark when you stopped or restarted, and see how your trend changed during each period.

Presets cover semaglutide (injection or pill), tirzepatide, liraglutide and orforglipron. Any other medication can be added by name.

Reminders that stay private

  • Weekly, daily or every-few-days reminders, with Taken and Snooze 1 hr right in the notification.
  • The wording is generic by default ("Time for your weekly check-in"). Your medication's name only appears if you choose to show it.
  • On the lock screen, the reminder just says "Reminder".

Doses and weight on the same chart

Your weight trend is the main line. Dose days, dose changes and the periods you were on a medication can be marked on the same chart, so you can see your whole log at a glance instead of flipping between apps.

About the estimated level

Lightarc can show an estimated medication level. It's worked out from the doses you logged and the average figures in each medication's published prescribing information, shown as a percentage of the highest estimate since your first logged dose.

It's an illustration, not a measurement. In the app's own words: "It's an illustration to help you picture your schedule. Don't use it to decide when or how much to take. Ask your prescriber."

What Lightarc doesn't do

"Not a medical device. Does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Talk to your prescriber about your medication."

Lightarc keeps your log. It doesn't interpret it, and it never tells you what to take or when.

Your log stays yours

  • No account and no sign-up. Your data stays on your phone.
  • Export your doses and side effects as CSV files, or everything as a full backup, any time.
  • Already using Shotsy? Lightarc imports Shotsy CSV files, including doses, side effects and weigh-ins, with a preview first.

Try it

Lightarc is a weight trend tracker for Android with GLP-1 dose logging built in. Logging, the trend chart, reminders, dose tracking, import and export are free. No account. No ads.

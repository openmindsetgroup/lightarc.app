How to move your weight history to a new Android app

Years of weigh-ins are worth keeping. If you're switching apps, or moving from an iPhone to Android, you don't have to start your chart from zero. This guide shows how to take your history out of Libra or Happy Scale as a CSV file and bring it into Lightarc.

Step 1: Export a CSV from your old app

From Libra (Android)

Use Libra's export option to save your weigh-ins as a CSV file, and keep it somewhere you can find it, such as Downloads or your Google Drive.

From Happy Scale (iPhone)

Happy Scale's export options are in its Settings, under Sync, Backup & Export. Export your weigh-ins as a CSV file and send it to yourself, for example by email or Google Drive, so you can open it on your Android phone.

Happy Scale's own .HappyScale complete backup file can't be imported. Use the CSV export.

From another app Lightarc can also import from Shotsy (doses, side effects and weigh-ins), from most other apps' CSV files, and from a Lightarc backup.

Step 2: Import it into Lightarc

You can import during setup or at any time later.

  • During setup: at the import step, choose From a file and pick your CSV.
  • Later: open Settings, then Your data, then Import.

Lightarc always shows you a preview before anything is saved, so you can check the dates and weights look right. Libra's body fat and muscle come along too. Files over 50 MB are refused.

Step 3: Check your trend

Once the import is saved, your whole history appears on the Trend tab as one smoothed line. Weigh-ins that came from a file are tagged "imported" in History, so you can always tell them from the ones you typed in.

From a smart scale

If your smart scale's app saves weigh-ins to Health Connect, Lightarc can read them (weight only, read-only). It's off until you turn it on, and it checks for new weigh-ins when you open the app.

Keeping your history safe from now on

  • No account. Your data stays on your phone.
  • Export any time: a full backup, or your weigh-ins as a CSV in kg or lb, from Settings → Your data → Export.
  • New phone: Android Backup to your own Google account is on by default (you can turn it off in Settings), and there's a "Moving to a new phone" help page in the app.
